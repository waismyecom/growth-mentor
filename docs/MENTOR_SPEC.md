Feature Specification: The "Ruthless Mentor" Interview Module
The core of this module is an interview engine powered by a "Ruthless Mentor" persona. Unlike traditional, sycophantic AI assistants designed for user comfort, this module is programmed to act as a high-stakes strategist.
- Heuristic for Conservatism: The system evaluates user inputs against industry benchmarks. If a goal is identified as "safe" or "easy," the AI is hard-coded to reject the input and trigger a "Push Back" sequence.
- Standard Enforcement: The AI enforces a "10x growth" standard across work, money, health, relationships, and growth.
- Vagueness Elimination: The system employs a "Zero-Tolerance for Abstractions" policy. Any input lacking a quantifiable metric or hard deadline is returned for revision.
Data Transformation: From Vague Dreams to Hard Targets
The system architecture employs a top-down hierarchical decomposition of user goals, transforming abstract desires into specific data points.
Temporal Mapping: Top-Down Hierarchical Decomposition
The system utilizes the 10-year vision as the parent node from which all subsequent milestones are derived. Through a recursive prompting strategy, the app programmatically deconstructs the 10-year dream into a 3-year milestone and a 1-year operational plan. This ensures that the user’s immediate trajectory is mathematically and strategically tethered to their long-term potential.
Establishing these high-level, rigorous goals necessitates a realistic assessment of the user's current starting point to identify the true gap between dream and reality.
2. The Diagnostic Layer: The Weekly "Truth" Scoreboard
The "Truth" Layer is the system's primary mechanism for combating the "Dunning-Kruger effect"—the cognitive bias where users subjectively rate themselves as "above average" despite objective stagnation. By replacing internal feelings with a weekly, data-driven scoring system, the app forces a confrontation with reality.
Metric Framework: The Seven Core Areas
The app tracks seven non-negotiable domains. A "10/10" is defined by specific, high-standard activities, not user sentiment.
1. Health: Hard physical training daily and strict nutritional adherence (e.g., maintaining a calculated caloric deficit).
2. Relationships: Quantifiable investment in professional and personal networks.
3. Love: Documented, intentional engagement with a primary partner.
4. Work: Completion of "needle-moving" activities defined in the 1-year plan.
5. Money: Tracking of specific revenue, savings, and investment targets.
6. Fun: Scheduled, high-quality rejuvenation time.
7. Personal Growth: Active, measurable skill acquisition and learning.
The Grading Logic: Objective Audit Protocol
Every Friday, the "Ruthless Coach" initiates a diagnostic interview. Crucially, the user is not permitted to self-grade, as the system assumes the user is "full of crap" and prone to bias. The AI conducts the audit based on "non-negotiable" activity logs. The system grades the user against their own 10-year standards, generating a scoreboard that highlights the two lowest scores for immediate corrective focus.
Analysis of Agreement Bias (Sycophancy Mitigation)
In alignment with the findings from the 2025 OpenAI update—where sycophantic, overly agreeable AI behavior was identified as a product failure—this app is programmed to "push back." The AI is prohibited from validating the user’s excuses. If the metrics aren't met, the AI reflects the failure accurately, prioritizing the "truth" as the only path to 10x growth.
Once the lowest scores are identified and the truth is established, the app must prevent the user from attempting to solve the "easy" version of those problems.
3. Root Cause Analysis: The "Double-Click" Diagnostic Module
The Root Cause Analysis module addresses the psychological phenomenon of "attribute substitution," where users swap a difficult question (the real bottleneck) for an easier one (a shiny new project). Solving the wrong problem faster is simply "failing at scale."
Protocol: The "Double-Click" Logic Flow
The system executes a recursive prompting sequence to bypass superficiality and identify the actual bottleneck:
1. Selection: User identifies a low-score area (e.g., Relationships).
2. Recursive Prompting: AI initiates "Double-Click" mode, asking multiple rounds of "Why" and "How" to probe beneath the initial answer.
3. Cross-Reference: AI analyzes responses against "Attribute Substitution" patterns to detect if the user is avoiding the difficult truth.
4. Identification: AI identifies the "Hidden Truth" (e.g., uncovering that a marriage issue is actually a cleanliness/responsibility issue).
5. Hard-Coded Output: To prevent decision paralysis, the system is constrained to output exactly one high-impact variable for the user to change.
Differentiator Identification: Superficial vs. Hidden Truths
Identifying the root cause is useless if the user lacks the "Loss Aversion" mechanisms to ensure execution.
4. Momentum Mechanics: Rewards, Stakes, and Visual Scorecards
Strategic use of "Loss Aversion" is the primary engine for retention. Humans are evolutionarily wired to do more to avoid a loss than to achieve a gain. A number on a spreadsheet lacks the emotional weight required to drive behavior during periods of low motivation.
The Stakes Framework: Emotional Weighting
The app requires users to define high-stakes consequences that cannot be "shrugged off."
- Rewards (Gains): High-value experiences (e.g., a Bali vacation or a luxury watch) to celebrate milestone achievement.
- Stakes (Losses): Extreme consequences for failure. Examples include "losing your spot on the team" or "selling the cars." The app specifically tracks high-value assets to ensure the pain of loss is visceral (e.g., selling the GT3 RS, Taycan, or GT4).
The Hierarchical Milestone System
Tracking is tiered to maintain constant alignment:
- Annual Level: The "Big Goal" tied to the primary reward and the high-stakes consequence (e.g., the car).
- Quarterly Level: Intermediate milestones and smaller rewards to maintain mid-range momentum.
- Daily Level: Binary tracking of specific activities (e.g., sales calls, steps, caloric intake) that mathematically lead to the annual goal.
Dashboard Specification: The Visual Scorecard
The central dashboard is designed to maintain constant emotional engagement through:
- Days Left in Year: A countdown timer to create healthy urgency.
- Quarterly Progress: Real-time percentage of milestone completion.
- Daily Alignment Status: A binary "On/Off Track" indicator based on the day's non-negotiables.
While AI manages the data and the "ruthless" logic, certain levels of accountability require a human element that technology alone cannot provide.
